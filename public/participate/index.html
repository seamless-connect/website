Early design · Free at the point of use · Community funded

Where things stand

Help shape what gets built.

We're in early design of the shared infrastructure and developer services. Registration distribution, provider integrations, DNSSEC automation and agent registry integrations—DNS-based and non-DNS-based—are proposed work—not a claim of production availability.

Design partners bring real workflows, help define requirements and validate integrations as they become available.

A public-good model

Free use. Voluntary support.

Inspired by Let's Encrypt: nonprofit infrastructure sustained by organizations that benefit from it. Using the tools does not require payment or sponsorship.

FAN (agenticnet.org), a 501(c)(3) that also hosts the MIT-originated Project NANDA, currently hosts the project.
